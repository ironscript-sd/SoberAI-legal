3. Nature of the App — Estimation Only, Not a Measurement

SOBERAI IS AN EDUCATIONAL AND INFORMATIONAL TOOL ONLY. IT IS NOT A BREATHALYZER, A MEDICAL DEVICE, A DIAGNOSTIC INSTRUMENT, OR A LEGALLY RECOGNIZED METHOD OF MEASURING BLOOD ALCOHOL CONCENTRATION.

3.1 What SoberAI does. SoberAI takes information you voluntarily provide — such as your age, sex, height, weight, drinks consumed, time of consumption, food intake, water intake, and medications — and produces an estimated Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC) value using the Widmark formula and similar publicly available models. The App may also produce an estimated time until your BAC is likely to fall below the United States legal driving limit (0.08%), and a separate estimated time until your BAC is likely to reach 0.00%.

3.2 What SoberAI does NOT do. SoberAI does not measure your actual BAC. It does not detect, sense, or test alcohol in your body. It cannot determine whether you are impaired. It cannot determine whether you are legally permitted to drive in any specific jurisdiction. It is not a substitute for a breathalyzer, blood test, urine test, field sobriety test, or any other scientifically validated measurement.

3.3 Inherent inaccuracy. The estimates produced by SoberAI are based on generalized formulas that cannot fully account for the unique biological, physiological, behavioral, and environmental factors that affect how alcohol is absorbed, distributed, metabolized, and eliminated by an individual human body. Factors that influence actual BAC but that SoberAI cannot measure or accurately predict include, without limitation:

  • Individual variation in liver function and metabolic rate.
  • Genetic differences in alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH) and aldehyde dehydrogenase (ALDH) enzymes.
  • Hydration level beyond what you self-report.
  • Stomach contents, type of food eaten, and rate of gastric emptying.
  • Hormonal fluctuations, menstrual cycle, and pregnancy status.
  • Medications, supplements, and recreational substances — including any not disclosed to the App.
  • Pre-existing or undiagnosed medical conditions (including but not limited to liver disease, diabetes, kidney disease, gastritis, and gastrointestinal disorders).
  • Body composition, including muscle-to-fat ratio.
  • Sleep, fatigue, stress level, and emotional state.
  • The actual alcohol content of the specific drinks you consumed, which often differs from standard estimates.
  • The rate, pattern, and timing of consumption beyond what you self-report.
  • Tolerance and frequency of prior alcohol consumption.
  • Inaccurate, incomplete, or false information that you provide to the App.
  • Any other factor not captured in the App's input fields.

3.4 Estimates may be wrong — sometimes significantly. Because of the factors described in Section 3.3, the BAC estimate and time-to-sober estimate produced by SoberAI may differ substantially from your actual BAC at any given moment. The App may overestimate or underestimate your BAC. The App may indicate that you are below the legal driving limit when in fact you are above it, or vice versa. You acknowledge and accept this inherent and unavoidable inaccuracy as a condition of using the App.
