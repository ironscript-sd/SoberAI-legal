Privacy Policy

SoberAI · Effective May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salar Saber ("we," "us," or "our"), the individual developer of SoberAI, collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you use the SoberAI mobile application (the "App"). By downloading or using SoberAI, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

SoberAI is intended for users aged 18 and older, in accordance with our Apple App Store age rating. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide Directly

When you use SoberAI, you may provide the following information:

  • Personal profile information: age, biological sex, height, and weight.
  • Session data: information you enter during a sobriety estimation session, including number and type of alcoholic drinks consumed, time period of consumption, time of last drink, food intake, water intake, and any medications you are currently taking (including prescription medication names you choose to enter).
  • Optional session labeling: a name or identifier if you choose to log a session for yourself or someone else.

You provide this information voluntarily to enable the App's core functionality. You may choose not to provide some information, but doing so may limit the accuracy of the App's estimates or prevent you from using certain features.

Note on sensitive data: Height, weight, age, alcohol consumption patterns, and medication information are considered sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws. We treat this data with heightened care and use it solely to provide the App's core sobriety estimation functionality. SoberAI does not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any secondary purpose.

b. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

When you use SoberAI, certain third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the App may automatically collect technical information about your device and usage. This may include device identifiers (such as the iOS Identifier for Advertisers, or IDFA), IP address, device model, operating system version, language settings, and information about advertisements shown to you. See Section 4 for details on each third-party service.

c. Information Sent for AI Processing

When you complete a sobriety estimation session, the content of your session — including your profile data (age, sex, height, weight), drinks consumed, food intake, time information, and any medications you have entered — is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our backend (hosted on Render) and processed by OpenAI's GPT-4o model to generate an estimate and recommendation.

OpenAI training data policy: OpenAI does not use API data submitted through SoberAI to train its models. This is governed by OpenAI's API data usage policies, which apply to all standard API traffic by default. You can review OpenAI's API and privacy terms at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Backend retention: Session content sent to our backend is processed transiently and is not retained after the session ends. We do not maintain logs of session content beyond what is necessary to deliver the response back to your device.

2. How Your Information Is Stored

Profile data on device. Your personal profile information (age, sex, height, weight) and your local session history are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. We do not maintain a copy of this profile data on any backend system.

This means:

  • Your profile data and session history stay on your device.
  • If you delete the App, this data is removed along with it.
  • We cannot recover your data for you (for example, if you lose access to your device).
  • Resetting your device or clearing the App's data will erase your information.

Session content during AI processing. As described in Section 1c, the content of an individual session is transmitted to our backend and to OpenAI for processing. This transmission is necessary to generate your result and is not retained after the session completes.

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App.
  • Personalize App features (for example, BAC estimates and recommendations based on your age, sex, height, weight, drinks consumed, food intake, and medications).
  • Process AI-driven sessions you initiate.
  • Display advertisements within the App.
  • Process in-app purchases through Apple.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security issues, and technical problems.

  • Render — render.com/privacy

Our backend is hosted on Render, which provides the infrastructure that routes session content to OpenAI for processing. Render does not retain session content after processing is complete.

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With the third-party SDKs and services described in Section 4 (advertising, payments, AI processing, hosting).
  • When required by law, subpoena, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.
